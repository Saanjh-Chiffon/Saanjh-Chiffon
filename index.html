Replacement & Return Policy

Our policy is designed to keep the replacement process simple and transparent.

1. No routine returns: We do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, colour preference, fit preference, styling preference, or an order placed incorrectly by the customer.

2. Replacement is available for genuine issues: If you receive a wrong, damaged, or defective product, you can submit a replacement request from My Orders.

3. Report promptly: Please raise the request as soon as possible after delivery. We recommend submitting it within 48 hours of delivery so the issue can be reviewed quickly.

4. Condition & evidence: The saree should be unused and in its original condition with tags/packaging where applicable. For damage or wrong-item claims, photos and an unboxing video may be requested.

5. Approval & stock: Every replacement request is reviewed by Saanjh. Replacement is subject to verification and stock availability.

6. If replacement is unavailable: If an approved replacement cannot be fulfilled, Saanjh will contact the customer regarding the available resolution, subject to applicable law.

7. Shipping: Once a replacement is approved, the replacement dispatch/pickup process will be communicated to the customer. Any applicable shipping charges will be communicated before they are charged.

8. Order cancellation: Cancellation requests should be made before the order is dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.

9. Questions / grievances: Email sanskritisinghthagele14@gmail.com with your order ID and issue details. Saanjh will review and respond to the grievance.

This policy is a customer-facing draft and should be reviewed for your business's final legal/compliance requirements before publication.
